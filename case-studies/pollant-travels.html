The brief

Stop competing on price. Start competing on identity.

Pollant Travels had been operating for years with no clear brand position. They competed on price — which is a race no travel company wins. Every discount eroded margins without building loyalty. They needed a reason for clients to choose them that wasn't just cost.

We built the strategic foundation that let them compete on experience and identity instead. That meant articulating what Pollant genuinely stood for, identifying the audience they could serve better than anyone else, and constructing a messaging framework that communicated that difference clearly across every touchpoint.

The strategy covered three markets, mapped the competitive landscape, and gave the leadership team a clear decision-making framework — a filter for what to say yes to, and what to walk away from. The result is a brand that knows who it is and who it's for.
