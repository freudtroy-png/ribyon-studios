The brief

A cinematic identity built for the world stage.

Pixelplus needed to compete with international videography studios. Clients were choosing competitors not because of weaker work, but because of the brand perception gap — Pixelplus looked local while their reel was global.

The identity had to feel cinematic, precise, and premium — not like a freelancer's portfolio. Every element needed to signal craft and intentionality. The logo, the typography, the colour system — each had to earn its place by communicating something specific about the studio's ethos.

We built a visual language rooted in precision and depth: a mark system drawn from the geometry of the lens, a palette that lives comfortably in dark and light environments, and guidelines that let a creative team maintain consistency across film, digital, and print.
