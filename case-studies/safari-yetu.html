The brief

A content system that runs without a designer on call.

Safari Yetu had the product — exceptional East African safari experiences — but their social presence didn't reflect it. Posts were inconsistent: different fonts, mismatched colours, no unified voice. The team was spending hours on content that looked like it had been made in a hurry.

They didn't need a social media manager. They needed a system — templates, rules, and a tone of voice guide that let anyone on their team produce content that felt on-brand, every time.

We built a full content system: 40+ Canva templates covering posts, carousels, stories, and reels covers. A colour and typography usage guide. A content category framework that told the team what to post, when, and why. Three months post-launch, their profile reach had grown 62% with no increase in posting frequency.
