Cookie Policy

How Ribyon Studios uses cookies on our website, what data they collect, and how you can control your privacy settings.

Effective Date: 1 January 2026

Ribyon Studios ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to transparency about how we collect and use data. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, the types of cookies we use on our website at ribyon.com (the "Site"), how you can manage your cookie preferences, and your rights under the EU ePrivacy Directive, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

By continuing to browse our Site, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy, unless you have adjusted your browser settings to reject them. If you have any questions, please contact us at studio@ribyon.com.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognise your device, remember your preferences, and collect information about how you interact with the site. Cookies are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide a better user experience.

Cookies can be first-party (set by the website you are visiting) or third-party (set by a domain other than the one you are visiting, typically by analytics or advertising services). They may be session cookies, which expire when you close your browser, or persistent cookies, which remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies only for purposes that help us deliver and improve our website experience. We categorise the cookies we use as follows:

Essential Cookies (Strictly Necessary)

These cookies are required for the Site to function correctly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and session handling. Without these cookies, some parts of the Site would not operate properly. Essential cookies do not require your prior consent under the ePrivacy Directive, but we still inform you about them.

Analytics and Performance Cookies

These cookies allow us to count visits, measure traffic sources, and understand how visitors interact with our Site. We use this data to improve performance, optimise content, and identify technical issues. The data collected is aggregated and anonymised wherever possible. Analytics cookies are subject to your consent.

Functional Cookies

Functional cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalisation, such as remembering your cookie consent preference so you are not prompted every time you visit. These cookies are set by us and may be considered essential for the features they support.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our Site are placed by third-party service providers on our behalf. We use the following third-party services:

Google Analytics. We use Google Analytics to collect aggregated, anonymised data about how visitors use our Site. Google Analytics sets the _ga, _ga_XXXXXXXXXX, _gid, and _gat cookies listed above. Google may transfer this data to servers in the United States and other jurisdictions. Google's ability to use and share information collected by Google Analytics is governed by the Google Privacy & Terms. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on.

Google Fonts. Our Site uses Google Fonts for typography. When you visit our Site, your browser may request font files from Google's servers, which may log your IP address and browser information. This is a standard practice for web fonts and does not involve persistent cookies. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Vercel Analytics. If our Site is hosted on Vercel, the platform may deploy its own privacy-preserving analytics that do not use cookies or collect personal data. Refer to Vercel's documentation for their cookie-free approach to analytics.

5. Your Cookie Choices and Consent

When you first visit our Site, a cookie consent banner is displayed. This banner informs you that we use cookies and provides options to accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or customise your preferences. By clicking "Accept All," you consent to the use of all cookies described in this policy. By clicking "Reject" or declining non-essential cookies, only strictly necessary cookies will be set.

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clearing your browser's cookies and reloading our Site to trigger the consent banner again, or through your browser settings as described below.

Under the GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive, you have the right to:

  • Withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
  • Request information about which cookies are active on your device.
  • Object to the use of non-essential cookies.
  • Request deletion of data collected by third-party cookies by contacting the respective provider.

6. How to Manage Cookies in Your Browser

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can choose to block or delete all cookies, block third-party cookies only, or receive a notification when a cookie is set. Below are links to the cookie management guides for the most popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge
  • Opera

You can also opt out of Google Analytics specifically by installing the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on, which prevents Google Analytics from sharing data about your visits.

Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of our Site. If you choose to block all cookies, some features may not work as intended, and you may need to re-enter preferences each time you visit.
