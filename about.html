Vision

To be the defining brand studio for African businesses going global.

We believe the world's most compelling brands of the next decade will come from Africa. We want to build them.

Mission

To give every leader the brand their conviction deserves.

Most great businesses are let down by ordinary branding. We exist to close that gap — with strategy, craft, and an obsession with what actually works.

Who we are

Not an agency. A studio.

Ribyon Studios was founded in Nairobi with a single conviction: that brand is the most underinvested asset in African business. Not because founders don't care — but because most of what's available is either too expensive, too generic, or too disconnected from the reality of building in this market.

We built something different. A studio that works the way great creative agencies in New York or London work — strategy first, craft second, results always — but priced, scoped, and built for founders and businesses here and across the continent.

We're small by design. Every project is led by a senior strategist and designer, not handed to a junior team. We take fewer clients to give more to each one. And we measure success not by how the work looks in our portfolio, but by what it unlocks for yours.

How we think

Three things we believe deeply.

Strategy without design is a document. Design without strategy is decoration. We insist on both.
A brand isn't what you say it is. It's what people feel when they encounter your name — and that feeling is either designed or accidental.
The best brands in the world don't try to appeal to everyone. They have the courage to mean something specific to someone specific.
Values

Four principles. No exceptions.

These aren't wall words. They're how we decide what to say yes to — and what to walk away from.

01

Conviction over trend

We don't chase styles. We build visual systems rooted in who you are, not what's popular right now. Trends age. Conviction doesn't.

02

Clarity over decoration

Every element earns its place. If it doesn't communicate something specific, it doesn't belong. Simplicity is the hardest thing to achieve.

03

Craft over convenience

We don't use templates. We don't cut corners. Every mark, every word, every decision is made deliberately — because your brand deserves better than shortcuts.

04

Results over portfolio

A beautiful brand that doesn't perform isn't a success. We measure what we do by what it unlocks for our clients — not how it looks in our portfolio.
