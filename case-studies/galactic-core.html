The brief

Make complex data feel effortless.

Tybrite Labs needed a front-end for their analytics platform that could present dense, real-time data without overwhelming the people who depend on it. The existing prototype was functional but unintuitive — users needed training just to navigate the dashboard.

We came in at the design stage and stayed through to deployment. The brief was clear: clean, fast, and built to scale. No unnecessary complexity, no visual noise. Every element on screen had to earn its place by serving the user's goal.

We delivered a component-based UI system, a full design language for the platform, and a hand-coded frontend that loads in under 1.2 seconds on a standard connection — without sacrificing the data density the product needed.
